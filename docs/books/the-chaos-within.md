## Where it sits in the series

*The Chaos Within* is where Arielle starts: in the middle of the noise, twenty
years before the canyon. It is the first book of the trilogy and the place to
begin.

It is followed by [*The Canyon Within*]({{ '/books/the-canyon-within/' | relative_url }}).
