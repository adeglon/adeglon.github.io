--- layout: default permalink: / og_image: /assets/hero/hero-ridge-og-1200.jpg --- {%- comment %} THE HERO, rebuilt 2026-09-02. It stopped being a photograph with type on it. Every version before this asked one daylight photograph to be both a picture and a background for six pieces of type - a wordmark, a tagline, four nav links, a fourteen-word claim and a book. Dark scrims made the photograph grey; a cream wash made the type readable and the photograph bland; and the tagline, whatever was done to it, ended up over the trees where nothing can be read. That is not a tuning problem, it is the wrong shape: white letters need an empty dark quarter of a frame to live in, and this frame has none. So the type gets its own ground. Night panel on the left carrying everything that has to be READ, at full contrast; the photograph on the right at full strength, undimmed, unfiltered, finally allowed to look like a photograph. Nothing overlaps, so nothing has to be compromised, and the claim can be as large as it deserves. Agnès' own picture: her sons walking down a ridge above Lake Lucerne. Swiss, deliberately - she is Swiss-American and writes about living between the two. It is also the only candidate that shows a PATH being walked. {%- endcomment %}
