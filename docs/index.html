About me

I was born and raised in Switzerland, and my curiosity has taken me from biochemistry to Traditional Chinese Medicine, from molecules to meridians, and eventually to storytelling.

Along the way, I've learned to look at life through different lenses, discovering how much there is to see when we remain open to more than one perspective.

I've always loved books, languages, and the outdoors. I draw inspiration from the unexpected places life takes us, the people we meet, and the questions that don't always have easy answers.

I'm the author of Once You Know: A Guide to Preserve Your Child's Health and the Kids' Questions About Life trilogy, published in English, French, and German.

I'm now working on my Within Series: The Chaos Within, The Canyon Within, and The Creation Within. Through fiction, I explore relationships, self-discovery, and the messy beauty of being human.

When I'm not writing, you'll likely find me hiking a trail, hanging upside down on aerial silks, or exploring life from yet another perspective.
