The author

Agnès Déglon-Fischer

I'm a Swiss-American writer with a background in biochemistry and acupuncture. My work bridges the worlds of science and storytelling, blending evidence-based health with holistic perspectives and lived experience. Whether unpacking the latest research, reflecting on body–mind–soul connections, or writing from the trail, I aim to make complex ideas accessible and meaningful.

And when I'm not writing about the serious stuff? I'm a mama, soul-searcher, and semi-functional adult, making sense of the mess through stories, soul, and humor.
