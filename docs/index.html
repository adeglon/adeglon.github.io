One photograph across the whole screen, in its own daylight, and the only type on it is the masthead - name, navigation, the five nouns. That is the settled answer after several rounds. Type in the middle or at the foot of this frame can only be made readable by darkening the picture, and every time the picture was darkened enough for a headline it stopped being welcoming. The sky at the top is the one place that gives type a ground for free: it holds no detail, so a light lean over it costs the photograph nothing. Everything that has to be READ at length - the claim, the book - is on the cream below, where it can be as large as it likes. Agnès' own picture: her sons walking down a ridge above Lake Lucerne. Swiss, deliberately - she is Swiss-American and writes about living between the two - and the only candidate that shows a PATH being walked. {%- endcomment %}
