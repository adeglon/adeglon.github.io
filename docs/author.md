I'm Agnès Déglon-Fischer, a writer with a background in biochemistry and
acupuncture. My work bridges the worlds of science and storytelling, blending
evidence-based health with holistic perspectives and lived experience. Whether
unpacking the latest research, reflecting on body–mind–soul connections, or
writing from the trail, I aim to make complex ideas accessible and meaningful.

And when I'm not writing about the serious stuff? I'm a mama, soul-searcher, and
semi-functional adult. For the past 13 years (unpaid, of course), I've been
turning tiny humans into decent-ish people while navigating hot flashes,
emotional flash floods, and the occasional identity crisis. This is the space
where I make sense of the mess, through stories, soul, sarcasm, and the sacred
art of laughing when everything burns.

## The Within Trilogy

*The Canyon Within* is drawn from my own midlife journey and a rim-river-rim
hike through the Grand Canyon. I live in Southern California with my husband and
two sons.
