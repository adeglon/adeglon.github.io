*The Unexpected Journey of a Little Starfish*

{% comment %} PHOTO: Starfish under a starry sky (AI-generated) {% endcomment %}

### 1 — The Shore

How do you invite someone into an ocean they haven't even noticed?

A little starfish rests where the waves meet the sand, watching the sun rise over a world it loves but doesn't quite belong to.

The last stars fade from the sky, and the ocean begins to glimmer in the growing light. Gentle waves roll toward the shore, washing over the little creature before retreating into the endless blue. Fine white sand clings to its tiny arms.

Everything here feels so solid, so tangible. Beautiful, yet somehow foreign.

The little starfish is fascinated by this world, by its colors, its textures, its sounds. And perhaps most of all, by its people.

Some walk past without noticing the little starfish. They barely notice the ocean, the sunrise, or the beauty unfolding around them.

Others stop for a moment, admiring the waves before continuing on their way.

A few venture closer, dipping their toes into the water, feeling the waves gently wash over their feet.

The little starfish watches them with wonder.

There is so much beauty here, and it longs to share it.

But how do you invite someone when they haven't even noticed you?

### 2 — The Waves

The little starfish knows the ocean, but it seems to know it differently from those standing on the shore.

It feels the movement of the water, the rhythm of the waves, the changing seasons, the endless ebb and flow of the tides. It senses the pull of the moon, the warmth of the sun, the quiet transition from day into night.

Everything moves, everything changes. That constant change is Life.

It knows these movements intimately. It knows energy in motion. It knows emotions.

Like waves, they rise and fall, they stagnate and flow. Sometimes the waters are calm and gentle, barely stirring the sand beneath them. At other times, the waves grow powerful, sweeping everything along with them. And the little starfish loves to play in them.

It tumbles and rolls, carried by the currents, surrendering to the movement, trusting the ocean even when it cannot see where the next wave will take it.

Sometimes the intensity frightens it. Sometimes it wishes it could stop the waves altogether.

But it knows the waves are not something to fight. They are something to feel, to move with, to experience.

Feeling deeply is both beautiful and frightening, but the little starfish wouldn't trade that feeling for a life spent watching the waves from the shore.

### 3 — The Ocean

The little starfish ventures deeper into the ocean. All around it, countless droplets of water melt into one another, becoming something so much greater than themselves. Where does one droplet end and another begin?

The little starfish lets itself drift into the vastness. It melts into movement and flow, until it can no longer tell where it ends and the ocean begins.

There is nothing to hold on to, nothing to possess, nothing to contain.

Beauty simply is.

It moves through everything, belongs to no one, and is there for anyone willing to experience it.

The little starfish feels it in the gentlest current, in the powerful waves, in the stillness beneath the surface.

Occasionally, it encounters another creature swimming in these depths, and for a moment, the wonder can be shared.

But such encounters seem rare.

So much beauty, so much intensity, and so little company with whom to share it.

But why?

### 4 — The Reflection

At night, the ocean becomes still.

The little starfish watches the moon reflected in the water. A million stars shimmer in the darkness, their light dancing softly across the waves.

For a moment, it is hard to tell where the ocean ends and the sky begins, and it knows that the ocean isn't the whole story.

It looks toward the sky above and feels the solid earth beneath it. Heaven and Earth. Yin and Yang. Opposite and complementary, forever connected.

And there, between them, the little starfish finds itself.

In the space where the visible touches the invisible, where the tangible world opens into something without boundaries.

The veil is thin here.

It knows this little body is only temporary, but the energy and love flowing through everything are timeless, limitless, infinite.

And as it gazes at the stars above and their reflections below, something stirs inside it. Something like a memory.

### 5 — Home

The little starfish wasn't always a starfish. Once, it was a little star, floating through the immensity of the cosmos, surrounded by love without boundaries.

There was no separation between itself and the countless stars dancing around it.

Love simply was… everywhere… and it was part of it all. One with everything.

Yet, it was curious to experience itself. And so, it descended into the solid world.

It crossed the invisible passage between Heaven and Earth, between the limitless and the tangible, and found itself in an ocean.

Here, love could be experienced through waves, movement, and touch. Through the warmth of the sun, the softness of the sand, the rhythm of the tides.

The little star had become a little starfish.

And as it explored this beautiful, unfamiliar world, it discovered something it hadn't expected.

Not everyone seemed to recognize the love that surrounded them. Perhaps they had simply forgotten.

So it spends its time on Earth trying to share something it remembers. Something from home.
